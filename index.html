Welcome to my portfolio!


I'm currently working as an AI Engineer Intern at EisnerAmper, where I'm building agents that help teams adopt and get more out of new technologies. My path to data science started in finance, but I quickly realized I was more interested in what happened before the numbers. Finance taught me how to make decisions with data; machine learning taught me how to build the systems that make those decisions possible.


These days, I'm usually building something, experimenting with a new idea, or learning something new. I'm excited by the chance to build things that are thoughtful, useful, and solve problems worth solving.
